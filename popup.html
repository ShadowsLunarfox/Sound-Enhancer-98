Sound Enhancer Help

Turn the web up to eleven.

1. Open a website that plays audio.
2. Click Sound Enhancer in the Chrome toolbar.
3. Choose Connect this tab and adjust the mixer.

100% is the original volume. 500% is 5× gain; 1000% is 10×. Bass and treble run from −12 to +12 dB. The limiter reduces peaks, so the perceived boost depends on the source.

Audio keeps playing when you close this window. Disconnect to restore normal website audio. Moving to a different website disconnects the tab.

Chrome internal pages and some protected media cannot be captured. This controls browser tabs, not Windows system volume.

All processing happens on your device. Audio is never recorded or uploaded.
